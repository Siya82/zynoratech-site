DashFactor: Math Runner

Think Fast. Move Smart.

Run through neon worlds, read the sum, and swipe into the gate with the right answer before you reach it. Each world teaches a new topic, from simple addition all the way up to exponents.

10

Worlds

From the City to the Galaxy, each with its own maths topic and look.

×5

Combo multiplier

Chain correct answers: ×2 after 3 in a row, rising to ×5 for long streaks.

3

Lanes, one swipe

Simple to play, hard to master. The pace rises the further you run.

25

Gates per world

Clear 25 gates to unlock the next world, with revision questions along the way.

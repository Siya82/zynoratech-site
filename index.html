About Zynora

Built in KwaZulu-Natal, for South Africa.

Zynora Technologies is a technology company based in Pietermaritzburg. We build products for sectors that matter to everyday South Africans, and we design for the conditions people actually live with.

That means apps that work on an ordinary Android phone, respect prepaid data, speak the learner’s home language, and protect personal information under POPIA.

  • Phone-first and data-lightInstallable apps that cache wisely and work on modest devices and networks.
  • Verified, not assumedFrom self-checking maths to tested question banks, we prove our products work.
  • Privacy by designPOPIA-aligned from day one. We collect only what a product needs.
  • Built to scaleShared foundations and open-source infrastructure keep costs low as we grow.

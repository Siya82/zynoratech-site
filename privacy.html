Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Zynora Technologies ("Zynora", "we", "us") handles information in the mobile game DashFactor: Math Runner ("the game") and on this website. We are based in South Africa and follow the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. Information you give us

The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

2. Information stored on your device

Your best score, unlocked worlds and sound setting are saved on your phone so you can carry on where you left off. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it.

3. Advertising

The game is free and is supported by ads, including optional rewarded ads a player can choose to watch (for example to continue a run). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which is certified for Google Play's Families programme.

  • Every player is treated as a child. All ad requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, so Google shows only non-personalised ads rated suitable for general audiences, with no interest-based advertising or remarketing.
  • The game does not request or send the device's advertising ID.
  • To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as IP address, approximate location (from the IP address) and device type, as allowed for child-directed ads.
  • Interstitial ads never appear when the game opens, only at natural breaks (after a run), and never in a player's first two sessions.
  • Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Analytics and crash reports

The game does not currently include any analytics or tracking tools. Google Play may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as install numbers and crash reports. If we add analytics in future, it will be configured for child-directed use, will not identify any player, and this policy will be updated first.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above, which process it under their own privacy policies, and where the law requires it.

6. Children

DashFactor is a maths game for players aged 7 and older, including children. We designed it to follow Google Play's Families policy, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR rules for children, and POPIA's protections for children's information.

  • The game has no accounts, chat or sharing features, and never asks a player for their name, email address, phone number, photos or location.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Progress is stored only on the device.
  • Ads are non-personalised and child-safe for every player (see section 3).
  • Parents and guardians can contact us at support@zynoratech.co.za with any question, or to ask us to delete any information a child may have sent us. We will respond within 30 days.

7. How long we keep information

We keep emails you send us for as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years. Our partners keep advertising and analytics data according to their own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. Email support@zynoratech.co.za. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. This website

This website does not use cookies, tracking or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.

DashFactor: Math Runner

How do I get to the next world?

Pass 25 correct gates. The counter under the question shows how many are left. Clearing a world also gives you a bonus and an extra life.

How does the combo work?

Answer correctly several times in a row to multiply your points: ×2 after 3, ×3 after 5, ×4 after 8 and ×5 after 12. A wrong answer resets the combo.

Why does the pause screen count down?

To keep things fair, the game is blurred while paused and resumes after 5 seconds. You get 3 pauses per run; pausing a 4th time restarts the run.

What does Continue do?

After a game over you can watch a short ad to keep running with one life. It costs you 2 gates of progress in the current world, and can be used once per run.

I lost my progress

Your best score and unlocked worlds are saved on your phone only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes them, and we cannot restore them.

How do I delete my data?

DashFactor does not have accounts and we do not keep your game progress on our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your phone. See our Privacy Policy for data handled by our advertising and analytics partners, and email us with any request.

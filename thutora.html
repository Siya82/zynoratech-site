Why THUTOra is different

Most AI tutors explain. THUTOra verifies and diagnoses.

Many tools claim step-by-step help. We protect two capabilities that make the help trustworthy.

Self-verified mathematics

MathGuard checks every step of a worked solution with a computer algebra system, so a learner sees when an answer has been verified and is warned when something doesn’t add up.

Root-cause diagnosis

Before teaching, THUTOra walks back through prerequisite topics to find where understanding actually broke down, then starts there.

Home-language help

Learners can ask for explanations in any of the 11 official languages, while subject terms and formulas stay in the exam language.

Made for phones

A topic drawer, a compact header and a maths keyboard on demand give the conversation two-thirds of a small screen. Installable, and light on data. Built for the device most learners actually have.

Textbook beside the tutor

Learners read the topic’s textbook chapter and ask the tutor about it side by side, and can type fractions, powers and roots straight into the question box.

POPIA-aligned

Personal information is handled carefully, parents are verified before they connect, and the app never caches personal data on the device.

Topic induction videos

Every topic opens with a one-minute video built on the 5 Ws: what it is, why it matters, where you see it, who uses it and when you need it, plus a worked example. Narrated in a South African voice, or muted with words on screen for class.

Safe for learners

The tutor stays on schoolwork and never asks for personal details. If a learner discloses harm, it responds kindly and points them to Childline 116, the police on 10111 and a trusted adult.

Help built in

A Help button in every product opens searchable guides. School staff log support tickets in the app and follow the reply there, and learners and parents are pointed to the right person first.

The THUTOra family

Five products. One foundation.

Every part of the education system sees the same learning data, through a product made for their role.

For learners

THUTOra LEARN

The AI tutor: adaptive lessons, practice, assessments and help at any hour.

For teachers

THUTOra TEACH

Class analytics, learner diagnostics, assignments with marks, and a daily view of who needs help.

For parents

THUTOra CONNECT

Verified parents follow their child’s progress and know when to encourage or step in.

For school management

THUTOra ADMIN

The principal’s and school admin’s workspace: learners, classes, staff, notices and a to-do list of what needs fixing.

For schools & officials

THUTOra INSIGHTS

School-to-national analytics on curriculum gaps and which interventions actually work.

THUTOra CORE is the shared data and services foundation underneath: one identity, one curriculum model, and one diagnosis engine, so every product improves together.

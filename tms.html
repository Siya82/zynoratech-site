Mobility & fleet software

Digitising South Africa’s minibus taxi industry.

What TMS does

Run the fleet on facts, not guesswork.

Fleet at a glance

Every vehicle, driver and route in one owner dashboard, with each owner seeing only their own fleet.

Compliance alerts

Enter a renewal date once. Licences, discs, insurance and services show GREEN, ORANGE or RED automatically.

Cash & fares

Log trips and fares, reconcile cash against expectations, and see income by day, week or month.

Fuel & maintenance

Track fuel, odometer readings, tyres and service schedules by date or kilometres, whichever comes first.

Live GPS tracking

Vehicle positions on an open-source map, fed by in-vehicle GPS trackers.

Rank dispatch

Rank managers run a live loading queue across vehicles from many owners, the way taxi associations already work.

Roadmap
